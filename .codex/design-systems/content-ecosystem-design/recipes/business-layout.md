# Recipe: Business Layout

## Load When
- The surface needs enterprise navigation, filters, sidebars, status tags, sample/media cards, or business tables.

## Public Components
- `Filter`
- `Navbar`
- `Sidebar`
- `Tag/Status`
- `Tag/Avatar`
- `Tag/Label`
- `Tag/Content Type Label`
- `Tag Radio`
- `Card Radio`
- `Sample Card`
- `Sample Card / Video&Photo`
- `Sample Card / Audio`
- `Sample Card / Comments`
- `Sample Card / Image Comments`

## Hard Rules
- Use `Sidebar` for contextual side navigation or settings panels when it matches the product pattern.
- Use `Filter` for dense filter rows.
- Use `Tag/Status` for workflow/system status; do not invent new status pills.
- Use Sample Card family for media/item review surfaces.
- Do not use internal sample-card subcomponents directly.

## Evidence
Record business component names/keys, layout region, and any fallback.
